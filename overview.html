COMPANY OVERVIEW

Building Trust, Creating Better Communities

RAFTAR Real Estate Pvt. Ltd. is one of the most trusted and rapidly growing real estate companies, delivering premium residential townships and land plotting projects. With more than a decade of experience, we have successfully completed multiple developments, helping thousands of families achieve the dream of owning a home. Our commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction has earned us a strong reputation across India.

Our Vision

To make quality housing and premium residential plots accessible to every family through affordable pricing, innovative planning, and sustainable development.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional value by maintaining the highest standards of honesty, transparency, professionalism, and customer-first service in every project.

Our Goal

To continue creating landmark communities while maintaining superior construction quality, ethical business practices, and contributing to society through meaningful initiatives.

Managing Director, Raftar Film City Limited

Mr. J. D. Verma is an accomplished entrepreneur, visionary business leader, and the Managing Director of Raftar Film City Limited. With extensive experience across real estate, infrastructure, finance, media, publishing, housing and institutional leadership, he has successfully transformed ambitious ideas into impactful enterprises that contribute to economic growth and nation-building.

He is the visionary force behind Shree Ram Film City, an integrated cinematic township envisioned as one of the world's most comprehensive destinations for filmmaking, tourism, hospitality, education and cultural experiences, strategically located in the sacred city of Ayodhya.
